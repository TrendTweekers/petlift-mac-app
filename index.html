PetLift for Mac

Your own pet, living on your Dock

PetLift lifts your own dog or cat out of a photo, right on your Mac, and puts them on top of your Dock. With PetLift Pro they wake up: they hop along the Dock, come over to your pointer, and jump when you click them.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email admin@stackedboost.com. You get an answer from the developer, usually within two working days.

Common questions

Which photos work best?
A photo where your pet stands out clearly from the background, with the whole pet in view. PetLift uses the same subject lifting as the Photos app. If it finds no pet, try a photo with more contrast between your pet and what is behind them.
Why is my pet asleep?
In the free version your pet sleeps on the Dock. PetLift Pro wakes them up so they walk, play and follow your pointer. With Pro, pets also nap by themselves after you step away from your Mac for a few minutes, and wake up when you come back. You can turn that off in the paw menu.
My pet walks backwards.
Open Manage Pets from the paw icon in the menu bar and press the flip button under your pet. PetLift cannot tell from a photo which way your pet looks.
Can I move my pet?
Yes. Drag your pet anywhere and let go: they drop back onto the Dock.
Pets cover something I need to click.
Turn off "Stay in front of windows" in the paw menu. Pets then stay behind your windows and show on the desktop. You can also hide all pets with the switch at the top of the paw menu.
How do I restore PetLift Pro on another Mac?
Open the paw menu, choose Wake Them Up, then Restore Purchase, with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
